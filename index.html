2. Information We Collect

Account Information (User-Provided):

  • Email address and Name / Display Name: Collected when you create an account or sign in using Firebase Authentication.
  • User Identifier (UID): A unique pseudonymous account ID assigned by Firebase to authenticate and associate your productivity records with your private account.

Personal Productivity Content (User-Generated):

  • Tasks, routines, recurring goals, milestones, notes, drawings, priority levels, and category labels created by you.
  • User-scheduled focus session durations and self-reflection progress entries.
  • Echo Pro entitlement status to unlock premium features.

Device Diagnostics & Crash Logs (Automatically Collected):

  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, stack traces, device model, Android OS version, and app performance metrics collected via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose unexpected crashes, fix bugs, and maintain app stability.
  • Installation ID: An app-specific installation identifier generated by Firebase strictly for crash analytics and notification dispatch.
Data We Never Collect: We do NOT collect GPS location, contacts, address book, phone records, SMS/text messages, camera feeds, microphone audio, photo library files, or Google Advertising IDs (AAID).

3. Focus Lock Permissions & Sensitive Access

Echo provides an optional self-discipline tool named Focus Lock that enables you to block access to apps you designate as distracting during scheduled focus sessions. To deliver this on Android, the app requests the following specific permissions:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS):
    Used solely to determine the package name of the active foreground application in real-time. Echo checks only whether a user-selected blocked application is running while an active Focus Lock session is in progress.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW):
    Used solely to draw the full-screen blocking overlay above a designated blocked app to prevent distraction and redirect you to your focus goal.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE):
    Category / Subtype: Enforce focus sessions by blocking distracting apps. Used strictly to ensure reliable session timing and active overlay triggering throughout your user-initiated focus window.
Explicit Clarification on Accessibility Service:
Echo: Life Tracker DOES NOT use the Android Accessibility Service API (AccessibilityService). Focus Lock relies strictly on standard Android UsageStatsManager and System Alert Window.
  • No Content Inspection: Focus Lock never reads, records, or transmits screen content, text, keystrokes, personal messages, web history, or account credentials.
  • Local Operation: Foreground app evaluation runs entirely locally on your device in real-time; no log of your app activity is transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Full User Control: Focus Lock is completely optional. You can grant, deny, or revoke these permissions at any time via Android System Settings without affecting core task or note-taking features.

4. Exact Alarm Permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM)

Echo utilizes Android's exact alarm permissions strictly for user-configured productivity schedules:

  • Triggering deadline notifications at the specific date and time selected by you.
  • Sounding critical alarms for recurring daily/weekly goals.
  • Accurately starting and ending user-scheduled Focus Lock windows.

This permission is not used for promotional messages, background tracking, or advertising.

5. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To save, organize, and synchronize your tasks, goals, and notes across your devices.
  • To authenticate your user account securely via Firebase Auth.
  • To enforce focus timers and display blocking overlays upon your request.
  • To send local reminder notifications and alarm prompts for scheduled tasks.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve application performance via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • To verify and maintain your Echo Pro subscription status.

No Selling or Advertising: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. Echo contains zero third-party advertisements and does not engage in behavioral profiling.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Echo integrates with trusted infrastructure partners to provide secure services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: User registration, login, and secure token verification.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud storage for your account profile, tasks, goals, and notes.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Diagnostic crash logging and performance monitoring to debug errors.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription management. All financial payments are handled directly by Google Play; Echo never collects or stores credit cards, bank accounts, or billing information.

7. Data Storage, Security & Access Rules

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between Echo and cloud databases are encrypted end-to-end using modern HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Strict Firestore Access Rules: Server-side security rules strictly restrict database read and write permissions: only the verified authenticated user (matching the authenticated request.auth.uid) can access or modify their personal records. No other user can view your data.
  • Local Storage: On-device data is stored safely in a private SQLite Room database protected by standard Android application sandboxing.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal productivity data (tasks, habits, goals, notes) only for as long as your account remains active and registered.

  • Active Account: Data remains stored to enable multi-device sync until you delete individual items or delete your account.
  • Upon Account Deletion: All user records, notes, goals, tasks, and cloud backups are permanently purged from Google Firestore servers immediately upon account deletion.
  • Crash Logs: Diagnostic reports and crash traces stored in Firebase Crashlytics are automatically deleted within 90 days.
  • Local Data: Local device storage can be erased immediately by uninstalling the app or clearing application data in Android Settings.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data and can permanently delete your account and all associated records anytime through two methods:

Option 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open Echo: Life Tracker on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your decision.

This instantly and irrevocably deletes your user profile, tasks, habits, goals, notes, and cloud database records from our servers.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app or device, you can request full account and data erasure by emailing us:

echolifetracker@gmail.com

Please specify your registered account email. All requests are verified and permanently fulfilled within 30 days.

Note: Active Google Play subscriptions must be canceled separately via Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & Subscriptions. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel Google Play recurring billing.
