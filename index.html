Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Your email address and name when signing in
  • A unique identifier for secure account management

App Content:

  • Tasks, goals, milestones, notes, and habits created in the app
  • Focus session schedules and progress statistics

Device Data:

  • Locally cached user data for fast offline access

We do not collect location data, contacts, phone calls, text messages, photos, or personal files.

Focus Lock and App Blocking Permissions

Echo includes an optional Focus Lock feature to help you avoid digital distractions by preventing access to designated apps during scheduled focus sessions.

  • What is checked: Only whether a chosen blocked application is opened while an active focus session is running.
  • What is never accessed: Keystrokes, passwords, screen text, messages, personal conversations, or browsing history.
  • Privacy safeguard: App checking runs locally on your device in real time. It is never uploaded, never saved to servers, and never shared.
  • Control: You decide which apps to block and can turn this feature on or off at any time in Android settings.
Focus Lock permissions are used strictly to enforce self-selected focus timers on your device. No data is collected through these permissions.

Subscriptions and Payments

Echo Pro subscriptions are handled securely through Google Play Billing.

  • All transactions are processed directly by Google Play. Echo never receives or stores payment cards, bank details, or financial accounts.
  • Your subscription status is stored to grant access to premium features.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play Store under Subscriptions.
Echo does not handle payment card numbers, banking passwords, or payment credentials.

How We Use Information

  • To save and sync your tasks, goals, and notes across your devices
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure
  • To enforce focus timers and app blocking when requested by you
  • To deliver notifications for scheduled reminders and deadlines
  • To maintain your Echo Pro subscription status

We do not sell your data, use your personal data for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Third-Party Services

Echo works with trusted infrastructure providers to deliver its services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Secure user sign-in and account verification
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud storage for your productivity data
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Anonymous diagnostic reports to fix app crashes and improve stability
  • Google Play Billing: Secure subscription management and payment processing

Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data and can delete your account and records at any time through two methods:

Delete Directly Inside the App

  1. Open Echo Life Tracker
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion

This instantly and permanently deletes your account, tasks, goals, notes, and cloud backups from our servers.

Email Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request full data erasure by sending an email to:

echolifetracker@gmail.com

Please mention your registered email address. We verify and complete account deletion requests within 30 days.

Account deletion is permanent. If you have an active subscription, remember to cancel it separately in Google Play Store under Subscriptions.

Children's Privacy

Echo Life Tracker is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal details, please contact us and we will promptly delete the data.

Data Security

We use standard encryption protocols to protect your information. All communication between the app and our database is encrypted in transit using secure HTTPS connections. Database access rules ensure that only you can access your personal data.
