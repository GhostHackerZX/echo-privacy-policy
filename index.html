Privacy Policy for Echo: Life Tracker

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Welcome to Echo: Life Tracker. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or log in (via Firebase Authentication), we collect basic user info such as your email address and profile identifiers.
  • User-Generated Content: Tasks, notes, and goals created within the app are stored locally on your device (using Room database) and optionally synced to Firestore cloud storage.
  • Usage & Diagnostic Data: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and performance data to improve application stability.

2. Accessibility Service Disclosure

Echo: Life Tracker uses Android Accessibility Services to assist users with automated task management and productivity features. Specifically:

  • The Accessibility Service is used solely to detect app launch context and assist in automating user-defined tasks.
  • We DO NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, text typed, screen content, or sensitive credentials accessed via the Accessibility Service.
  • Using this service is strictly voluntary and requires explicit user consent within the app settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Echo application.
  • Synchronize your tasks, goals, and notes across authorized devices.
  • Fix bugs, crashes, and technical issues.

4. Data Deletion & Account Erasure

You can delete your account at any time directly inside the app under Profile Screen > Delete Account. Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes all your personal data from Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and your local device storage.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Crashlytics): For authentication, cloud database, and crash logging.
  • Google Play Billing: For secure in-app purchases.
